Where does everything go?

The profile
Stays in this browser until you close it. A download contains encrypted data; saving on this device is optional.
The passphrase
Stays with you. Keep it separately from the file. If it’s lost, this example cannot recover the profile.
A copied selection
Goes to your clipboard only after review. Your device and the assistant you paste it into then control that copy.
Encryption and the limits of this example

AES-256-GCM, a fresh salt and nonce per export, and PBKDF2-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations protect the versioned JSON file. JSON is also a YAML 1.2 subset. A wrong passphrase or changed ciphertext cannot unlock it.

The browser makes no model or storage-server requests. An optional server-side home-page count includes no profile or visitor details. Clearing this view cannot guarantee erasure from browser or operating-system memory. Device compromise, extensions, cross-device recovery and independent security review remain unresolved.

This demonstration carries assistant context. It does not sign you into App Composer or provide a shared login for games. Provider behaviour can differ. No real private records, including Emilio data, are used or supported.
