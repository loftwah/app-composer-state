Original App Composer illustration — Carry / forward
Reviewed 17 October 2026.

Source: one generated raster, "tile-carryforward-v5", produced through the fal.ai queue endpoint
openai/gpt-image-2.5/sunburst/text-to-image under MP's authorisation, request
01a14914-3913-7531-86aa-9703b16d14d4. The prompt, the full job record and the export commands are
recorded in the owning App Composer repository at .artwork/tiles-v5.json,
.artwork/masters/tile-carryforward-v5.result.json and .artwork/export-v5.sh. FAL_AI_API_KEY is read
from the local environment by scripts/generate-app-art.mjs; it is never printed, never written to a
manifest, never committed and never shipped to the browser.

Art direction (supersedes the rejected 17 October cut-paper set): original adult-animated science
fiction with confident dark ink contours, hard-edged cel shading in two flat tone steps, drawn
perspective and one clear focal event. Scene: a workshop with two visibly different machines — a
bulky retro mainframe with reel-to-reel tapes on the left, a slim modern handheld on a cradle on
the right — while a short four-armed alien courier carries a distinctive memory core (dark shell,
glowing violet centre, brass contacts) between them. A continuous violet signal ribbon runs from
the mainframe through the carried core into the handheld, so the transfer is happening as the core
moves. A small maintenance bot sleeps on top of the mainframe. Entirely illustrated; no photograph,
no 3D render, no glow haze and no text baked into the image.

Files in this repository, all exported from the master at review time:
- carry-forward-art-v4-master.png — the reviewed master, square; byte-identical to the reviewed
  .artwork/masters/tile-carryforward-v5.png recorded by App Composer.
- carry-forward-art-v4-480.webp and carry-forward-art-v4-960.webp — 480px and 960px page hero
  derivatives with a reserved 1:1 layout box.
- carryforward-share-v4.jpg — 1200x630 JPEG social derivative, composed with deterministic
  typography from the reviewed master. The same bytes are this app's card in the App Composer
  catalogue.
- carryforward-share-v4.webp and carryforward-share-v4-600.webp — 1200x630 and 600x315 WebP
  variants.
- apps/carryforward-small-v4.webp — 96px navigation crop of the same artwork.

The illustration is explicitly separate from the fictional vault: it is never presented as a
screenshot, as real assistant memory or as a real person's data.

mark.svg and icon.png are authored, not generated: hand-written SVG geometry in the same family
grammar (ivory tile, ink border, one ink subject and one accent — here a dark memory canister with
a violet core), plus a 512px rendering of that mark for the apple-touch icon.

Superseded in this release and removed from the repository: the 17 October 2026 cut-paper
carry-forward-art-v3 master, 480/960 derivatives, share cards, 96px crops and the v1 suitcase tile.
Their bytes and prompts remain in this repository's git history.
