Original App Composer cut-paper artwork — Carry / forward
Reviewed 17 October 2026.

Source: one generated raster, "tile-carryforward-v3", produced through the fal.ai queue endpoint
openai/gpt-image-2.5/sunburst/text-to-image under MP's authorisation, request
01a147dc-918d-74e0-a209-ddc9eab7db05. The request itself, its prompt, the cost basis and the
review of every rejected image are recorded in the owning App Composer repository at
public/brand/apps/provenance.json and .artwork/tiles-direction-a.json. FAL_AI_API_KEY is read
from the environment by scripts/generate-app-art.mjs; it is never printed, never written to a
manifest, never committed and never shipped to the browser.

Art direction: App Composer cut-paper screenprint — warm ivory paper #f4f0e8, graphite ink
#252923, burnt orange #d95022, with violet #8b5cf6 as this app's accent. Flat shapes, ink-lined
paper edges, coarse screenprint grain, deliberate registration offset. No shading, perspective,
gloss, gradients or glow. Subject: a small graphite travelling case sitting on a bold violet
route line that runs from lower left, bends once at a right angle and continues right, with two
ivory paper tags threaded onto it — exactly one filled solid burnt orange and one left plain, so
one is clearly chosen and one is not. A thin graphite handle arcs over the case; no lock and no
key appear anywhere, because nothing here is locked. The case and tags stand for context you
choose to carry forward; they are never presented as a screenshot, a vault or a real record.

Files in this repository, all exported from the master at review time:
- carry-forward-art-v3-master.png — the reviewed master, square (1024px); byte-identical to the
  reviewed .artwork/masters/tile-carryforward-v3.png recorded by App Composer.
- carry-forward-art-v3-480.webp and carry-forward-art-v3-960.webp — 480px and 960px page hero
  derivatives, one square tile in a reserved 1:1 box with a 1px hairline border.
- carryforward-share-v3.jpg — 1200x630 JPEG social derivative. The same bytes are this app's card
  in the App Composer catalogue (public/brand/apps/carryforward-share-v3.jpg there), so the
  product page and the catalogue describe the app with one card.
- carryforward-share-v3.webp and carryforward-share-v3-600.webp — 1200x630 and 600x315 WebP
  variants.
- apps/carryforward-small-v3.webp — 96px navigation crop of the same square artwork, alongside
  the other four apps' crops for this app's own app switcher.

Dimensions are reserved in the markup. The illustration is explicitly separate from the example
profile: it never depicts a real memory, a real person or a claim that anything was shared.

mark.svg and icon.png are authored, not generated: hand-written SVG geometry of the case, route
line and chosen tag in the same paper grammar, plus a 512px flat ivory rendering of that same
mark for apple-touch-icon. Before this release both files, at both public/mark.svg and
public/brand/mark.svg, were byte-identical to App Composer's own mark and icon, so the app wore
the parent product's logo; this release gives it its own. No traced third-party mark, vendor icon
or platform logo is used.

Superseded in this release and removed from the repository: the glossy carry-forward-share-v2.jpg
social card and its provenance JSON, which showed a keepsake box with a key; the v1 state-v1.webp
hero and its provenance JSON; the unreferenced share-ai-v1 poster pair; the unused cover.png
social duplicate; and the duplicated copies of the bundled font and its OFL licence that sat at
the repository root as well as under public/brand. Their bytes and prompts remain in this
repository's git history; the reviewed App Composer record keeps the comparison note.
